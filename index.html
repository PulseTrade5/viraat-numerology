Report kitne samay mein milti hai?

Booking ke baad, aapki personalised report generally 24-48 ghante ke andar WhatsApp ya email par bhej di jaati hai.

Kya naam badalna zaroori hai naam correction ke liye?

Nahi. Zyaadatar cases mein sirf spelling mein chhota sa badlav sujhaya jaata hai — aapka official naam wahi rehta hai, sirf likhne ka tareeka thoda adjust hota hai.

Consultation call kitni der ki hoti hai?

Package ke hisaab se 30-60 minute ki hoti hai, jisme aapke sawalon ka vistrit jawab diya jaata hai.

Nahi, hamare upay hamesha vyavahaarik aur bina todphod ke hote hain — chhote changes se hi positive asar dikhta hai.

Payment kaise kar sakte hain?

Booking form bharne ke baad hum WhatsApp par sampark karke payment aur further process detail se samjha dete hain.

Kya online consultation available hai?

Ji haan, saari consultations WhatsApp call/chat ke through online hoti hain — aap kahin se bhi connect ho sakte hain.
